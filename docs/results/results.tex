\paragraph{Structure-fidelity metrics.} All operate on the trained embeddings
$h_v$ with hyperbolic distance $d_{\mathcal{H}}$; pair/anchor quantities are
sampled with a fixed seed.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{depth\_radius\_rho} --- whether embedding norm grows with tree
        depth; the Spearman correlation between $\lVert h_v\rVert$ and depth over
        all non-root nodes.
  \item \texttt{dist\_tree\_rho} --- whether embedding distance tracks tree
        distance; the Spearman correlation between $d_{\mathcal{H}}(h_u,h_v)$ and
        tree distance over sampled node pairs.
  \item \texttt{recon\_map} --- whether a node's direct tree neighbours are its
        nearest points; the mean Average Precision of ranking parents$+$children
        by $d_{\mathcal{H}}$ over sampled anchors.
  \item \texttt{subtree\_ap} --- whether same-subtree nodes cluster together; the
        mean Average Precision of ranking same-level-$k$-ancestor nodes by
        $d_{\mathcal{H}}$ over sampled anchors.
  \item \texttt{ancestor\_auc} --- whether ancestors are closer than unrelated
        nodes; the AUC separating (node, ancestor) pairs from (node, random) pairs
        scored by $-d_{\mathcal{H}}$.
  \item \texttt{sibling\_ratio} --- whether siblings are tight without collapsing;
        the mean sibling-pair $d_{\mathcal{H}}$ divided by the mean random-pair
        $d_{\mathcal{H}}$.
\end{itemize}
