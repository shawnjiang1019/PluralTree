READ THE GATE LOG IN THIS ORDER (it stops at the first failure)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. headroom     union@8 - within-answer on the 7B base. <= 0.03 -> RL is over,
                  exits immediately. GRPO can only reweight what the base already
                  samples; no spread means nothing to select.
  2. flat groups  share of questions where all 8 rollouts get identical rewards.
                  Those z-score to zero advantage and train nothing. Fail > 0.30.
  3. judge vs itself   must exceed 0.5, else there is no stable ordering to track.
  4. confirm-half concordance + CI vs the bar, and the lambda=0 control (does the
                  novelty term add agreement over plain coverage?).
